How it works

From one number to a whole website.

Your ISBN already knows your title, cover, blurb and publisher. We use it to build your site, so you start with something finished — not a blank page.

1

Enter your ISBN

ISBN-13 or ISBN-10, and we find the book, its cover and your other titles. Kindle-only? Paste your Amazon link and add the cover and blurb yourself.

2

Pick a look

Choose one of nine templates. Match your cover's colours in one click, or pick a palette — light or dark, four font pairings each.

3

You're live

Add a headshot and bio whenever you're ready. Every new book you publish slots right in.

Templates

Nine templates. Nine different shapes.

Not one layout in nine colours: each is built differently, around a different idea of what your site should lead with. All nine look finished with nothing but your ISBN, and you can switch whenever you like without losing a thing.

Every picture is the real template, shown with an invented author and invented books. Yours will have your covers and your words, and any template can take its colours from your cover.

What's on your site

Everything a reader looks for.

Every book, every store

A page for each title with buy links to Amazon and Kindle, Bookshop.org, Barnes & Noble, Apple Books and Kobo. In Amazon's Associates program? Add your tag and those links earn you commission too.

A reading order

Give your books a series and number, and readers get a page showing where to start and what comes next.

Reviews, all in one place

Your best praise on each book's page, and gathered on a Reviews page of its own.

Excerpts

Let readers try the opening pages, a chapter or a poem right on the book's page.

Events

Signings, launches, panels and livestreams, soonest first. Past events file themselves away.

A blog

News and letters to readers. Keep a post as a draft, or date it ahead and it appears on that day.

Your own pages

For book clubs, press, a FAQ, or anything else. In your menu, or private to whoever has the link.

Your newsletter list

Readers sign up right on your site, and the list is yours to download. Already on Substack, Kit or Mailchimp? Send them there instead.

Your name, your way

Upload your signature or logo, or sign your name in a handwritten typeface. Your colours can come straight from your cover.

About & contact

Your bio, author photo and a contact page for readers, press and events.

Your own address
